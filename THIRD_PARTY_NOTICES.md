# Third-party notices

Some skills here adapt passages from other people's work. Each passage names its source in a credit line where it is used. This file collects the license and copyright notice those licenses ask to travel with the copies. The repo's own `LICENSE` (MIT) doesn't cover these passages; each keeps its author's license.

Third-party skills installed unmodified by `bin/fetch-third-party.sh`, or committed with their own `LICENSE` file, are listed in the README's "Third-party skills" table and carry their own notices.
